Sophia — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Who uses this app

Sophia is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not available to the public.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Google Calendar and Gmail data through Google's official APIs. This is used only to read, organize, and draft calendar events and email for the owner.

How the data is used and stored

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. Sign-in credentials are stored encrypted on that computer. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
