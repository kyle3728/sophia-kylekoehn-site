Kyle Media

Kyle Media is Kyle Koehn's private toolkit for audiobooks, narrated tutorials, and personal music projects. It is used by its owner and is not offered as a public service.

The toolkit prepares narration and video, keeps selected project files in the owner's private Google Drive media workspace, and delivers finished audio to the owner's YouTube account as private videos.

With the owner's approval, separate Google connections provide access to Drive files created or opened through the app and to the owner's YouTube videos and playlists. YouTube permissions include managing videos, playlists, and configured revision cleanup. Uploads are private by default.

Jobs can run on the owner's computer or on private cloud workers configured by the owner. This website contains app information only; it does not provide a sign-in service or host private media.
